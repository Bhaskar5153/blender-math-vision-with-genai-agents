🎞️ **Blender Animation Plan: Solving a Cubic Equation**

**Overall Theme:** A dynamic whiteboard in a minimalist classroom setting. Equations and numbers manifest as glowing 3D objects, operations are represented by active symbols and movements.

---

### 1. 🎞️ Step-by-Step Animation Plan

**Scene 1: Introduction - Presenting the Problem (0-5 seconds)**
*   **Action:**
    *   Camera smoothly pans into a clean `WhiteboardGrid` in a `ClassroomScene` (soft focus on background elements like a desk).
    *   Title "Solving Algebraic Equations" fades in at the top of the whiteboard.
    *   The original equation `(x^2 - x) = (x^3 - 1)` animates into existence, term by term, from left to right.
        *   `VariableBox(x^2)` (red square plane) slides in.
        *   `OperationArrow(-)` appears and connects to `VariableBox(x)` (blue cube).
        *   `EqualsBar(=)` appears at the center.
        *   `VariableBox(x^3)` (green cube) slides in.
        *   `OperationArrow(-)` appears and connects to `ConstantBlock(1)` (white block).
*   **Focus:** Clearly present the initial problem.
*   **Camera:** Static shot, slightly angled to show depth of objects.

**Scene 2: Rearranging Terms to Zero (5-15 seconds)**
*   **Action:**
    *   Text "Step 1: Move all terms to one side." appears below the equation.
    *   The left side `(x^2 - x)` highlights with a subtle glow.
    *   An `OperationArrow(-)` representing "subtracting `(x^2 - x)`" flies from the left side, across the `EqualsBar`, and hovers over the right side.
    *   `VariableBox(x^2)` (red square) on the left side fades out and a new `VariableBox(-x^2)` (red square with a glowing minus sign) simultaneously slides in on the right side, just after `x^3`.
    *   `VariableBox(-x)` (blue cube with minus) on the left side fades out and a new `VariableBox(+x)` (blue cube with a glowing plus sign) simultaneously slides in on the right side, just after `-x^2`.
    *   The `EqualsBar` shrinks slightly as a `ZeroBlock(0)` appears on the left side.
    *   The terms on the right ($x^3, -1, -x^2, +x$) automatically reorder themselves in descending power: `x^3 - x^2 + x - 1`.
    *   The final equation for this step, `x^3 - x^2 + x - 1 = 0`, is now displayed clearly.
*   **Focus:** Visualize the balancing act of moving terms across the equals sign.
*   **Camera:** Gentle pan to follow the movement of terms.

**Scene 3: Factoring by Grouping - Part 1 (15-25 seconds)**
*   **Action:**
    *   Text "Step 2: Factor the polynomial." appears.
    *   The equation `x^3 - x^2 + x - 1 = 0` remains.
    *   `Parentheses` animate to group the first two terms: `(x^3 - x^2)`. They glow.
    *   `VariableBox(x^2)` (red square) highlights as the common factor within `(x^3 - x^2)`.
    *   An `OperationArrow(Factor Out)` (glowing 'x^2' symbol) shoots out from the group.
    *   `VariableBox(x^3)` visually "sheds" an `x^2` dimension to reveal an `x` inside new `Parentheses`. `VariableBox(-x^2)` visually "sheds" an `x^2` dimension to reveal a `-1` inside the same new `Parentheses`.
    *   Result: `x^2(x - 1)` forms.
    *   The `OperationArrow(+)` between the groups lights up.
    *   `Parentheses` animate to group the last two terms: `(x - 1)`. They glow.
    *   `ConstantBlock(1)` highlights as the common factor.
    *   An `OperationArrow(Factor Out)` (glowing '1' symbol) shoots out from the group.
    *   Result: `+ 1(x - 1)` forms.
    *   The full line: `x^2(x - 1) + 1(x - 1) = 0` is now visible.
*   **Focus:** Illustrate how factoring works by visually "extracting" common terms.
*   **Camera:** Zoom in slightly on the grouping, then zoom out to show the full line.

**Scene 4: Factoring by Grouping - Part 2 (25-30 seconds)**
*   **Action:**
    *   Both `(x - 1)` `Parentheses` terms glow simultaneously.
    *   One `(x - 1)` `Parentheses` term floats to the left, taking its place as the new common factor.
    *   The remaining `x^2` and `+1` terms smoothly combine and animate into new `Parentheses` right after the `(x - 1)`: `(x^2 + 1)`.
    *   The full line: `(x - 1)(x^2 + 1) = 0` is now displayed.
*   **Focus:** Show the final step of factoring by grouping.
*   **Camera:** Tight shot on the terms as they re-combine.

**Scene 5: Solving Each Factor - Case 1 (Real Solution) (30-38 seconds)**
*   **Action:**
    *   Text "Step 3: Set each factor equal to zero and solve." appears.
    *   The equation `(x - 1)(x^2 + 1) = 0` splits into two pathways.
    *   Camera focuses on the left path: `x - 1 = 0`.
    *   `ConstantBlock(-1)` glows.
    *   A `OperationArrow(+)` with a `ConstantBlock(1)` (representing '+1 to both sides') shoots from the right side of the `EqualsBar`, hitting `ConstantBlock(-1)`.
    *   `ConstantBlock(-1)` fades out, and `ConstantBlock(1)` appears on the right side of the `EqualsBar`.
    *   The equation transforms to: `x = 1`.
    *   The `VariableBox(x)` and `ConstantBlock(1)` become solution blocks and gently float down into a "Solution Zone" at the bottom of the screen.
*   **Focus:** Clear step-by-step solving for the first factor.
*   **Camera:** Split screen or quick pan between paths.

**Scene 6: Solving Each Factor - Case 2 (Complex Solutions) (38-50 seconds)**
*   **Action:**
    *   Camera shifts focus to the right path: `x^2 + 1 = 0`.
    *   `ConstantBlock(+1)` glows.
    *   A `OperationArrow(-)` with a `ConstantBlock(1)` (representing '-1 to both sides') shoots from the right side of the `EqualsBar`, hitting `ConstantBlock(+1)`.
    *   `ConstantBlock(+1)` fades out, and `ConstantBlock(-1)` appears on the right side of the `EqualsBar`.
    *   The equation transforms to: `x^2 = -1`.
    *   A `SquareRootSymbol` animates into existence and hovers over both sides of the equation.
    *   `VariableBox(x^2)` visually "de-squares" into `VariableBox(x)`.
    *   `ConstantBlock(-1)` visually transforms into `ImaginaryUnit(i)` with a `PlusMinusSymbol(±)` appearing before it.
    *   The `PlusMinusSymbol(±)` splits `ImaginaryUnit(i)` into two distinct blocks: `ImaginaryUnit(i)` and `ImaginaryUnit(-i)`.
    *   The equation transforms to: `x = i` and `x = -i`.
    *   These two `ImaginaryUnit` solution blocks gently float down into the "Solution Zone" with `x = 1`.
*   **Focus:** Explain complex numbers visually and simply.
*   **Camera:** Remains focused on the current path, zooming in/out as needed.

**Scene 7: Final Solutions & Summary (50-60 seconds)**
*   **Action:**
    *   Camera pulls back to reveal all three solution blocks (`x=1`, `x=i`, `x=-i`) arranged neatly in the "Solution Zone."
    *   Text "Final Answer:" appears above them.
    *   Each solution block pulses gently.
    *   The `WhiteboardGrid` highlights the path taken from the initial equation to the final solutions.
    *   Fade to black.
*   **Focus:** Consolidate the solutions and provide a sense of completion.
*   **Camera:** Wide shot showing the entire whiteboard.

### 2. 🧱 Asset Suggestions

*   **VariableBox(x):** A sleek, slightly metallic blue cube.
*   **VariableBox(x^2):** A flat, vibrant red square plane, subtly extruded to indicate depth.
*   **VariableBox(x^3):** A larger, translucent green cube, appearing more volumetric than `x`.
*   **ConstantBlock(N):** A clean, matte white rectangular block with the number 'N' extruded from its face.
*   **ZeroBlock(0):** A transparent, light grey sphere with a bold '0' at its core.
*   **OperationArrow(Symbol):** A glowing, thin arrow.
    *   `+`: Cyan glow.
    *   `-`: Magenta glow.
    *   `*` (implied by factoring): Yellow glow.
    *   `Factor Out Symbol` (e.g., `x^2` or `1` floating at the arrow head): Pulsing green glow.
*   **EqualsBar(=):** Two parallel, glowing white lines that can dynamically resize.
*   **Parentheses:** Smooth, rounded, semi-transparent grey brackets that can animate to enclose objects.
*   **ImaginaryUnit(i):** A stylized, floating 'i' symbol, perhaps with a slight ethereal, purplish aura. When split, they are identical, one with a glowing `-` prefix.
*   **PlusMinusSymbol(±):** A small, dynamically appearing symbol, glowing white.
*   **SquareRootSymbol:** A dynamic stroke that forms the radical symbol, glowing lime green.
*   **Background:**
    *   `WhiteboardGrid`: A large, clean, slightly reflective white board material with a subtle light grey grid pattern.
    *   `ClassroomScene`: A minimal background blurred, suggesting a learning environment (e.g., a simple desk with a lamp, a window showing soft light, but not distracting).
*   **Text elements:** Clean, modern sans-serif font (e.g., Montserrat or Open Sans), slightly extruded for a 3D effect, white or light grey.

---

### 3. ⏱️ Timing and Transitions

*   **Smooth Motion:** All object movements (sliding, flying, rotating) should be interpolated using smooth curves (e.g., Bezier or Ease In/Out) for a professional, unjarring feel.
*   **Fading:** Objects appearing or disappearing should use a quick, smooth fade-in/fade-out effect.
*   **Glow & Highlight:** Active elements (terms being operated on, common factors, operation arrows) should glow briefly to draw attention, then return to normal.
*   **Duration:** Each major step (Scene 1-7) is allocated roughly 5-10 seconds, with complex factoring/solving steps taking slightly longer.
*   **Camera:** Gentle, purposeful camera movements. Avoid rapid cuts. Pans and zooms should be smooth and deliberate, focusing on the active area of the whiteboard.
*   **Sound (Implied):** Subtle, clean sound effects for object appearance, movement, and glows would enhance clarity, though not explicitly requested, they are standard in cinematic animations.
