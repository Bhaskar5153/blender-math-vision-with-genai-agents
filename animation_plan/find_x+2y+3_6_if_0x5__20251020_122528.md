🎞️ **Animation Plan: The Impossible Condition**

**Scene 1: Introduction - The Challenge Appears**
*   **Visual:** Camera slowly pans across a clean, well-lit whiteboard surface. A faint grid pattern is visible on the whiteboard.
*   **Text (fade in):** "Elementary Algebra Challenge!"
*   **Visual:** The problem statement ($x + 2y + 3 = 6$ and $0 > x > 5$) materializes letter by letter, then number by number, onto the whiteboard. The equation is centered, with the condition below it.
*   **Text (fade in):** "Find $x + 2y + 3 = 6$ if $0 > x > 5$"
*   **Camera:** Gentle zoom on the entire problem.
*   **Sound:** Soft, inviting synth music begins.

**Scene 2: Focusing on the Condition**
*   **Camera:** Zooms in sharply on the condition "$0 > x > 5$". The rest of the problem fades slightly into the background.
*   **Visual:** The text "$0 > x > 5$" glows with a soft, analytical blue light.
*   **Text (fade in):** "First, let's carefully analyze the condition for X."
*   **Sound:** A soft "ding" as the text glows.

**Scene 3: Deconstructing the Condition - Part 1 (x < 0)**
*   **Visual:** The condition "$0 > x > 5$" fluidly separates into two distinct expressions: "$0 > x$" and "$x > 5$". They drift apart slightly on the whiteboard.
*   **Camera:** Focuses on "$0 > x$".
*   **Visual:** A `VariableSphere(x)` appears, labeled 'x'. A `ConstantBlock(0)` appears, labeled '0'. An `OperationGlow(<)` ( '<' symbol) appears between them, showing `x < 0`.
*   **Text (fade in):** "`0 > x` means X is strictly less than 0."
*   **Sound:** Gentle whoosh as expressions separate, then a soft hum for the glowing operator.

**Scene 4: Deconstructing the Condition - Part 2 (x > 5)**
*   **Camera:** Smoothly slides to focus on "$x > 5$". The previous `VariableSphere(x)` and `ConstantBlock(0)` fade out.
*   **Visual:** The `VariableSphere(x)` reappears. A `ConstantBlock(5)` appears, labeled '5'. An `OperationGlow(>)` ('>' symbol) appears between them, showing `x > 5`.
*   **Text (fade in):** "AND `x > 5` means X is strictly greater than 5."
*   **Sound:** Another gentle whoosh and hum.

**Scene 5: The Number Line Visualization - Introduction**
*   **Visual:** The whiteboard background transforms into a precise, illuminated `NumberLineDynamic`. Key integer markers (e.g., -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6) are clearly visible. The number line extends slightly beyond these points.
*   **Text (fade in):** "Can one X satisfy BOTH conditions simultaneously?"
*   **Camera:** Pans slightly to show the full extent of the relevant number line.
*   **Sound:** A thoughtful, questioning musical motif plays.

**Scene 6: Number Line Visualization - X < 0**
*   **Visual:** A shimmering, translucent **blue region** expands on the `NumberLineDynamic`, highlighting all numbers from negative infinity up to (but not including) 0. An open circle appears at 0.
*   **Visual:** The `VariableSphere(x)` appears within this blue region, subtly glowing.
*   **Text (fade in):** "Condition 1: X is less than 0."
*   **Sound:** A shimmering sound as the region appears.

**Scene 7: Number Line Visualization - X > 5**
*   **Visual:** The blue region for `x < 0` gently fades out.
*   **Visual:** A shimmering, translucent **red region** expands on the `NumberLineDynamic`, highlighting all numbers strictly greater than 5. An open circle appears at 5.
*   **Visual:** The `VariableSphere(x)` attempts to appear within this red region.
*   **Text (fade in):** "Condition 2: X is greater than 5."
*   **Sound:** A different shimmering sound, slightly more urgent.

**Scene 8: Number Line Visualization - The Contradiction**
*   **Visual:** Both the **blue region (x < 0)** and the **red region (x > 5)** attempt to appear simultaneously on the `NumberLineDynamic`.
*   **Visual:** There is a clear, unhighlighted gap between 0 and 5. The regions **do not overlap**.
*   **Visual:** The `VariableSphere(x)` tries to appear in the non-existent overlapping area, glitches, flickers rapidly, and then shatters into tiny glowing particles, dissolving.
*   **Visual:** A large, bold, red "IMPOSSIBLE!" text explodes onto the screen, accompanied by a vibrating "NO OVERLAP!" below it.
*   **Text (fade in):** "There is NO REAL NUMBER X that can be both less than 0 AND greater than 5 at the same time."
*   **Sound:** A sharp, conclusive "shatter" sound followed by a deep, resonant "THUD" for "IMPOSSIBLE!". Music becomes more serious.

**Scene 9: Conclusion on X's Condition**
*   **Visual:** The `NumberLineDynamic` fades out, returning to the clean whiteboard. The bold "IMPOSSIBLE!" remains for a moment, then also fades.
*   **Visual:** The separated conditions "$0 > x$" and "$x > 5$" briefly reappear, then a red X mark appears over them, and they dissolve.
*   **Text (fade in):** "Therefore, the condition $0 > x > 5$ is a **contradiction**." (The word "contradiction" glows intensely red).
*   **Text (fade in):** "This means there are NO possible values for X."
*   **Sound:** A final, definitive "whoosh" as elements dissolve.

**Scene 10: Back to the Original Equation (Briefly)**
*   **Camera:** Pans back to the full original problem statement ($x + 2y + 3 = 6$ if $0 > x > 5$). The condition part is now faded or crossed out, indicating its impossibility.
*   **Visual:** The equation part ($x + 2y + 3 = 6$) glows, but with a questioning, somewhat dim light.
*   **Text (fade in):** "If there's no X that fits the condition, then..."

**Scene 11: Final Solution - No Solution**
*   **Visual:** The entire problem statement on the whiteboard dissolves into individual glowing particles, then vanishes.
*   **Visual:** A large, prominent, clear "NO SOLUTION" message fades in, centered on the screen. It glows with a confident, final white light.
*   **Visual:** Below it, a subtle "Under the given conditions." disclaimer appears.
*   **Sound:** The music swells to a final, conclusive chord.

🧱 **Asset Suggestions:**

*   **Background:** `ClassroomWhiteboard` or `CleanWhiteboardGrid` (dynamic, can change to number line).
*   **Variables:** `VariableSphere(x)`, `VariableSphere(y)` - Smooth, labeled 3D spheres, perhaps slightly reflective.
*   **Constants:** `ConstantBlock(0)`, `ConstantBlock(3)`, `ConstantBlock(5)`, `ConstantBlock(6)` - Solid, floating numeric blocks.
*   **Operations & Relations:**
    *   `OperationGlow(+)`, `OperationGlow(=)`: Animated glowing symbols.
    *   `OperationGlow(>)`, `OperationGlow(<)`: Dynamic, showing relation.
    *   `EqualsBarAnimated(=)`: A visual bar to show equality (though not extensively used for simplification here).
*   **Number Line:** `NumberLineDynamic` - An interactive number line that can:
    *   Show markers (-1, 0, 1, ..., 5, 6).
    *   Highlight ranges with specific colors (blue for x<0, red for x>5).
    *   Display open circles at boundaries (0 and 5).
*   **Text Elements:** Clear, readable 3D text for problem, conditions, explanations, and conclusions. Can be animated with fade-in, glow, or dissolve effects.
*   **Effects:** Shimmering lights for regions, particle shatter for `VariableSphere(x)`, glowing effects for emphasis, "X" mark overlay for impossible conditions.

---

⏱️ **Timing and Transitions:**

*   **Overall Pace:** Moderate, allowing time for comprehension at each logical step.
*   **Scene 1 (Intro):** 5-7 seconds. Slow pan, gradual text reveal.
*   **Scene 2 (Focus Condition):** 3-4 seconds. Quick zoom, text glow.
*   **Scene 3-4 (Deconstruct):** 4-5 seconds each. Fluid separation, variable and block appearance, text fade-in.
*   **Scene 5 (Number Line Intro):** 4-5 seconds. Whiteboard transformation, line appearance, thoughtful text.
*   **Scene 6-7 (Number Line Regions):** 5-6 seconds each. Region expansion, variable appearance, text.
*   **Scene 8 (Contradiction):** 8-10 seconds. Simultaneous region attempt, lack of overlap, variable glitch/shatter, "IMPOSSIBLE!" explosion. This is the core visual climax.
*   **Scene 9 (Conclusion X):** 5-6 seconds. Fade outs, "contradiction" glow, final text.
*   **Scene 10 (Back to Eq):** 2-3 seconds. Quick pan, equation glow.
*   **Scene 11 (Final Solution):** 5-7 seconds. Problem dissolve, "NO SOLUTION" reveal, final chord.
*   **Transitions:** Primarily smooth camera pans, gentle zooms, and controlled fade-in/fade-out for objects and text. Effects like glowing, shimmering, and shattering are used for emphasis.
*   **Sound:** Subtly used to enhance transitions, emphasize key revelations (like the contradiction), and maintain an engaging atmosphere.
