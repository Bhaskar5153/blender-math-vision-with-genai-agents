# 2. 🧱 Asset suggestions

*   **WhiteboardBG:**
    *   **Mesh:** Large, flat plane.
    *   **Material:** `Principled BSDF` with a clean, slightly glossy white color (RGB: 0.95, 0.95, 0.95), `Roughness` 0.6. A very subtle, faint grid pattern using a `Mix Shader` with a `Light Gray` `Emission` node and a `Checker Texture` as a `Fac`, set to a low `Emission Strength` for a subtle grid glow.
    *   **Lighting:** Soft, even overhead area lights to simulate classroom lighting, avoiding harsh shadows.
*   **VariableCube(label):**
    *   **Mesh:** A simple cube, slightly chamfered edges.
    *   **Material:** Semi-transparent, light blue/cyan `Principled BSDF` (Base Color: `0.1, 0.5, 0.7`, `Metallic`: 0.1, `Roughness`: 0.2, `IOR`: 1.4, `Transmission`: 0.8).
    *   **Label:** A `Text` object parented to the cube, white or slightly emissive (emission strength 1.0-2.0). Font: clean, sans-serif (e.g., Open Sans, Montserrat).
*   **ConstantBlock(value):**
    *   **Mesh:** Rectangular prism.
    *   **Material:** Opaque, matte light grey `Principled BSDF` (Base Color: `0.7, 0.7, 0.7`, `Roughness`: 0.8).
    *   **Value:** `Text` object extruded on its front face, dark grey or black.
*   **OperationArrow(symbol):**
    *   **Mesh:** A sleek, elongated arrow shape with a small triangle head.
    *   **Material:** Highly emissive `Principled BSDF` (Base Color: `0.8, 0.8, 0.1` - bright yellow, `Emission Strength`: 5.0-10.0).
    *   **Symbol:** `Text` object (e.g., '+', '-', 'x', '÷', '=') attached to the arrow's head, glowing with the same emissive material.
*   **EqualsBar / NotEqualsBar:**
    *   **Mesh:** A thin, horizontal rectangular prism. `NotEqualsBar` adds a diagonal prism.
    *   **Material:** Emissive `Principled BSDF` (e.g., purple/blue: `0.5, 0.1, 0.9`, `Emission Strength`: 5.0).
*   **FractionLine:**
    *   **Mesh:** A simple, thin line object.
    *   **Material:** White, subtly emissive (emission strength 0.5). Can be animated with a `Build Modifier` (for drawing) or `Trim Curve` for curves.
*   **CancelXEffect:**
    *   **Animation:** A glowing 'X' shaped mesh scales up quickly, pulses once, and then rapidly scales down and fades out.
    *   **Material:** Bright, ephemeral glow (e.g., red/orange emissive).
*   **HoleEffect:**
    *   **Mesh:** A small, slightly concave disc or sphere.
    *   **Material:** Dark, almost black, with a very subtle inner glow or edge highlight to make it noticeable. Can pulse its size slightly.
*   **GraphAxes:**
    *   **Mesh:** Two `Curve` objects (X and Y axis) with `Bevel` applied for thickness. Small tick marks as separate meshes.
    *   **Material:** Dark grey or black, solid.
    *   **Labels:** Small white `Text` objects (x, y).
*   **FunctionLine:**
    *   **Mesh:** `Curve` object animated with a `Trim Curve` modifier to draw itself along the path of $f(x) = x/4 + 3$.
    *   **Material:** Vibrant, emissive (e.g., neon green or bright blue, `Emission Strength`: 3.0-5.0).
*   **TextDisplay:**
    *   **Mesh:** `Text` objects.
    *   **Font:** Consistent, clear, sans-serif (e.g., Lato, Open Sans, Montserrat).
    *   **Material:** White, slightly emissive (emission strength 0.5-1.0) for clarity on the whiteboard.

# 3. ⏱️ Timing and transitions

*   **Overall Pace:** Moderate and deliberate, allowing sufficient time for viewers to read and comprehend each algebraic transformation. Avoid rushing.
*   **Scene Transitions:**
    *   **Camera Dolly/Pan/Orbit:** Smooth, cinematic camera movements (e.g., slow dollies forward/backward, gentle pans left/right, subtle orbits for perspective shifts) between major sections (simplification, domain, root).
    *   **Fade-in/out:** For text titles and major asset groups (e.g., problem statement, graph axes). Use `Material Alpha` or `Dissolve Modifier` for smooth transitions (0.75-1.0 seconds).
*   **Object Appearance/Disappearance:**
    *   **Fade/Dissolve:** Primary method for elements appearing or disappearing, creating a clean look (0.5-0.75 seconds).
    *   **Pop/Scale:** For very minor temporary elements, a quick scale-up/down can be used (0.2-0.4 seconds).
    *   **Slide-in/out:** For `TextDisplay` titles or summary points, to give a sense of organization and flow (0.5-0.75 seconds).
*   **Operational Animations:**
    *   **Highlight/Glow:** Elements currently being operated on should glow briefly (0.5 seconds pulse).
    *   **Arrow Movement:** `OperationArrow` assets should move decisively and smoothly between their points of operation (0.5-0.8 seconds).
    *   **Transformation:** The process of old elements being replaced by new, simplified elements should be clear and concise. This typically involves old elements fading out while new ones fade in or slide into position (1.0-1.5 seconds total for the transformation).
    *   **Calculations:** Visual representation of calculations (e.g., `0 - 3 = -3`) should be quick and snappy, with numbers replacing previous values (0.5 seconds).
*   **Pauses:**
    *   After the problem statement is fully displayed (2-3 seconds).
    *   After each major result (simplified function, domain statement, final root), hold the scene for 2-4 seconds for viewer comprehension.
    *   Brief pauses (1-2 seconds) after each sub-step to allow the viewer to process the change.
*   **Graph Drawing:** The `FunctionLine` should animate smoothly, drawing itself over 2-3 seconds, with the `HoleEffect` appearing immediately as it reaches $x=0$.
*   **Total Animation Duration:** Approximately 1 minute 45 seconds to 2 minutes 15 seconds, depending on the exact pacing of pauses and animations.
