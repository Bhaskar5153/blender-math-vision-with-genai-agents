Here's a detailed cinematic Blender animation plan for simplifying the expression `4(2x+3)-5x`, designed for elementary algebra students.

---

### 🎞️ Step-by-Step Animation Plan

**Overall Scene Setting:** A clean, slightly textured `WhiteboardGrid` background, with soft classroom lighting. The camera focuses on the center of the whiteboard.

---

**1. Introduction: The Problem (0 - 5 seconds)**

*   **Action:**
    *   **0-2s:** A large, stylized title "Simplifying Algebraic Expressions" fades in, then fades out.
    *   **2-5s:** The problem statement "Problem: simplify 4(2x+3)-5x" slides in from the top.
    *   **5s:** The expression `4(2x+3)-5x` materializes on the whiteboard from left to right, piece by piece, using the defined assets.
        *   `ConstantBlock(4)` appears.
        *   `Parentheses(` appears.
        *   `ConstantBlock(2)` and `VariableBox(x)` appear, linked.
        *   `OperationSymbol(+)` appears.
        *   `ConstantBlock(3)` appears.
        *   `Parentheses)` appears.
        *   `OperationSymbol(-)` appears.
        *   `ConstantBlock(5)` and `VariableBox(x)` appear, linked.
*   **Visuals:** Each element appears with a satisfying 'pop' and slight glow.

---

**2. Step 1: Distribute the 4 (5 - 25 seconds)**

*   **Action:**
    *   **5-7s:** The `4` and `(2x+3)` part of the expression (i.e., `4(2x+3)`) gets a `HighlightGlow`. A `TextLabel("Step 1: Distribute")` appears briefly above it.
    *   **7-10s:** The `ConstantBlock(4)` subtly detaches and moves slightly to the left. Two shimmering `OperationArrow(x)` visual effects (like light trails) emanate from `ConstantBlock(4)`, one arcing towards `2x` and the other towards `3`.
    *   **10-14s (Distribute to 2x):**
        *   The `OperationArrow(x)` going to `2x` brightens.
        *   `ConstantBlock(4)` and `ConstantBlock(2)` (from `2x`) slide together.
        *   They pulse, `ConstantBlock(2)` shrinks, `ConstantBlock(4)` transforms into `ConstantBlock(8)`. The `VariableBox(x)` from `2x` slides to reattach to the new `ConstantBlock(8)`.
        *   The original `ConstantBlock(2)` fades out. Result: `8x`.
    *   **14-18s (Distribute to 3):**
        *   The `OperationArrow(x)` going to `3` brightens.
        *   `ConstantBlock(4)` (a duplicate, or the original returns and moves) and `ConstantBlock(3)` slide together.
        *   They pulse, `ConstantBlock(3)` shrinks, `ConstantBlock(4)` transforms into `ConstantBlock(12)`.
        *   The original `ConstantBlock(3)` fades out. Result: `12`.
    *   **18-20s (Reassemble):** The `OperationSymbol(+)` from inside the parentheses reappears between the newly formed `8x` and `12`.
    *   **20-25s (Update Expression):**
        *   The original `4(2x+3)` section on the whiteboard smoothly collapses and is replaced by `8x + 12`.
        *   The full expression now dynamically updates to `8x + 12 - 5x`.

---

**3. Step 2: Combine Like Terms (25 - 45 seconds)**

*   **Action:**
    *   **25-27s:** The terms `8x` and `-5x` get a `HighlightGlow`. A `TextLabel("Step 2: Combine Like Terms")` appears briefly.
    *   **27-30s (Identify):** The `VariableBox(x)` elements in both `8x` and `-5x` pulse with a shared, distinct color (e.g., green), indicating they are "like." The `ConstantBlock(12)` remains still, showing it's different.
    *   **30-33s (Group):** `8x` and `-5x` smoothly slide closer together, centered. The `ConstantBlock(12)` shifts slightly to the right to accommodate. A soft, transparent `GroupingBox` subtly appears around `8x - 5x`.
    *   **33-38s (Perform Operation):**
        *   The `OperationSymbol(-)` between `8x` and `5x` glows intensely.
        *   `ConstantBlock(8)` separates from its `VariableBox(x)`, and `ConstantBlock(5)` (from `-5x`) also separates.
        *   `ConstantBlock(8)` and `ConstantBlock(5)` slide together, and the `OperationSymbol(-)` appears between just them.
        *   The `ConstantBlock(5)` subtly subtracts from `ConstantBlock(8)` (e.g., `8` momentarily shows `7, 6, 5...` then settles on `3`).
        *   `ConstantBlock(5)` fades out, `ConstantBlock(8)` transforms into `ConstantBlock(3)`.
        *   The new `ConstantBlock(3)` slides to reattach to the `VariableBox(x)` that was previously with `8x` (or a new one appears if the old one faded). Result: `3x`.
    *   **38-42s (Update Expression):**
        *   The `GroupingBox` fades out. The original `8x` and `-5x` fade out.
        *   The newly formed `3x` slides into their position.
        *   The full expression now dynamically updates to `3x + 12`.

---

**4. Step 3: Final Simplified Expression (45 - 55 seconds)**

*   **Action:**
    *   **45-48s (Final Check):** `3x` and `ConstantBlock(12)` get a soft `HighlightGlow`. The `VariableBox(x)` on `3x` flashes. A 'No Variable' icon briefly appears next to `ConstantBlock(12)`. A `TextLabel("Can't Combine Further")` appears briefly.
    *   **48-52s (Final Answer):** The `HighlightGlow` fades. The entire expression `3x + 12` moves to the center of the whiteboard and scales up slightly.
    *   **52-55s:** A prominent `EqualsSymbol` appears below `3x + 12`. The words "Final Answer:" slide in from the left, aligning with the expression.
*   **Visuals:** A sense of completion and clarity.

---

**5. Outro (55 - 60 seconds)**

*   **Action:**
    *   **55-58s:** The entire whiteboard fades slightly, leaving only "Final Answer: `3x + 12`" sharply visible.
    *   **58-60s:** Camera pulls back slowly, then fades to black.

### 🧱 Asset Suggestions

*   **Background:**
    *   `WhiteboardGrid`: A clean, slightly reflective white surface with faint, light-grey grid lines. Could have a subtle texture suggesting marker smudges.
*   **Variables:**
    *   `VariableBox(x)`: A sleek, translucent green or blue cube with a prominent 'x' etched or projected onto its visible faces. It subtly glows. When combined, the 'x' might glow brighter.
*   **Constants:**
    *   `ConstantBlock(number)`: Solid, opaque blocks with rounded edges. Colors could vary:
        *   `4, 2, 3, 5`: Yellow or light orange.
        *   `8, 12, 3`: Slightly darker orange or red-orange, indicating a derived value.
        The number is clearly etched on its face.
*   **Operations:**
    *   `OperationSymbol(+, -, x)`: These are not physical objects but glowing, animated symbols that appear between terms. They pulse when active.
    *   `OperationArrow(x)`: Used specifically for the distributive property. These are shimmering light trails or particle effects that arc from one object to another, indicating a mathematical operation is about to occur.
*   **Parentheses:**
    *   `Parentheses(` and `)`: Thin, glowing arcs or shapes that enclose expressions. They could pulse when their enclosed expression is being focused on.
*   **Equality:**
    *   `EqualsSymbol`: A sleek, glowing horizontal bar. Perhaps it vibrates slightly when it appears, emphasizing balance.
*   **Visual Cues:**
    *   `HighlightGlow`: A soft, pulsing glow (e.g., golden or light blue) around the active elements or entire expressions.
    *   `GroupingBox`: A transparent, faintly glowing bounding box (e.g., light purple) that appears around terms being grouped.
    *   `TextLabel`: Clean, sans-serif font, perhaps slightly translucent, that appears and fades for instructions (e.g., "Distribute", "Combine Like Terms").
    *   `No Variable Icon`: A small, transparent gray 'x' with a diagonal line through it, briefly appearing to show a constant cannot combine with a variable.

### ⏱️ Timing and Transitions

*   **General:**
    *   **Smooth Easing:** All object movements (slides, scales, rotations) should use smooth ease-in/ease-out transitions for a professional, non-jerky feel.
    *   **Consistent Speed:** Maintain a steady pace for operations, allowing viewers to process each step.
    *   **Pauses:** Incorporate brief pauses after each significant transformation to let the result sink in.
*   **Object Appearance/Disappearance:**
    *   **Fade-in/out:** For symbols and labels.
    *   **Transform/Morph:** When numbers combine (e.g., 4 and 2 become 8), animate the `ConstantBlock` morphing rather than just disappearing and reappearing.
    *   **Shrink/Expand:** Elements can briefly shrink or expand when activated or deactivated.
*   **Highlighting:**
    *   Start highlighting *before* the action begins to draw attention.
    *   Increase glow intensity during the active operation.
    *   Fade out glow as the operation concludes.
*   **Camera:**
    *   Subtle zooms and pans to keep the active area in focus without being disorienting.
    *   A slight 'float' to the camera can add to the cinematic feel.
*   **Sound (Implied):**
    *   Soft 'whooshes' for object movements.
    *   Gentle 'chimes' or 'pops' for object appearances/transformations.
    *   A satisfying 'ding' for the final answer.

This plan aims to provide a clear, engaging, and educationally effective animation for the given algebraic problem.
