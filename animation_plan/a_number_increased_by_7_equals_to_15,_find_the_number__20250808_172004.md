### 🎞️ Step-by-Step Animation Plan

**Overall Scene Concept:**
The animation takes place on a large, clean digital whiteboard within a subtly blurred classroom background. The camera remains mostly static, focusing on the whiteboard, occasionally panning or zooming slightly to highlight details. All elements are 3D and have a soft, inviting glow.

---

**Phase 1: Introduction & Problem Statement (The Unknown)**

1.  **Scene Setup (0-3s):**
    *   **Visual:** Fade in to a crisp, slightly textured `WhiteboardGrid` with a blurred `ClassroomScene` background.
    *   **Motion:** A gentle hum of ambient classroom sound (optional).
2.  **Problem Introduction (3-8s):**
    *   **Visual:** A shimmering `QuestionMarkIcon` (translucent, slightly wobbly) floats into the center of the whiteboard.
    *   **Text Overlay:** "a number increased by 7 equals to 15, find the number" (Fades in below the question mark).
    *   **Motion:** `QuestionMarkIcon` gently pulses.
3.  **Define the Variable (8-13s):**
    *   **Visual:** The `QuestionMarkIcon` smoothly dissolves, and in its place, a glowing blue `VariableBox(x)` materializes and settles slightly left of the whiteboard center.
    *   **Text Overlay:** "Let 'x' = the unknown number." (Appears cleanly below the `VariableBox(x)`).
    *   **Motion:** `VariableBox(x)` gives a soft, confident pulse.

---

**Phase 2: Translating Words into an Algebraic Equation**

1.  **Building the Left Side (13-18s):**
    *   **Visual:** `VariableBox(x)` is already present.
    *   **Motion:** A vibrant green `OperationArrow(➕)` slides in from the right and snaps into place immediately to the right of `VariableBox(x)`. It briefly brightens. Then, a translucent orange `ConstantBlock(7)` slides in from the right and snaps next to the `OperationArrow(➕)`.
    *   **Equation Formed:** `x + 7` (visually on the whiteboard).
2.  **Introducing Equality (18-22s):**
    *   **Visual:** A pair of shimmering, gold `EqualsBar(=)` lines appear horizontally, perfectly centering themselves after the `x + 7` expression. They briefly glow.
    *   **Motion:** A translucent blue `ConstantBlock(15)` slides in from the right and settles to the right of the `EqualsBar(=)`.
    *   **Equation Formed:** The full equation `x + 7 = 15` is now clearly displayed.
3.  **Equation Confirmation (22-26s):**
    *   **Text Overlay:** "x + 7 = 15" (Appears neatly below the visual representation of the equation).
    *   **Motion:** The entire equation `x + 7 = 15` on the whiteboard gently pulses as if confirmed.

---

**Phase 3: Solving the Equation for 'x'**

1.  **Goal Emphasis (26-29s):**
    *   **Visual:** A subtle spotlight effect shines on the `VariableBox(x)`.
    *   **Text Overlay:** "Our goal: Isolate x!" (Fades in briefly above `x`).
2.  **Applying Inverse Operation (29-35s):**
    *   **Visual:** Two sets of operations appear simultaneously:
        *   A red `OperationArrow(➖)` and a `ConstantBlock(7)` slide in *below* the `+ 7` on the left side, slightly offset downward to indicate the next line of calculation.
        *   Another red `OperationArrow(➖)` and a `ConstantBlock(7)` slide in *below* the `15` on the right side.
    *   **Motion:** A faint, dashed line appears horizontally below both sides of the equation to visually separate the new step. The `OperationArrow(➖)` symbols briefly glow.
3.  **Simplifying the Left Side (35-39s):**
    *   **Visual:** The `ConstantBlock(7)` (positive) and the newly added `ConstantBlock(7)` (negative) on the left side glow intensely.
    *   **Motion:** They rapidly spin towards each other, collide, and then *dissolve* with a quick "poof" particle effect, leaving only the `VariableBox(x)` behind. The dashed line extends to just below `x`.
4.  **Simplifying the Right Side (39-44s):**
    *   **Visual:** The `ConstantBlock(15)`, the `OperationArrow(➖)`, and the `ConstantBlock(7)` on the right side glow.
    *   **Motion:** The `ConstantBlock(7)` (negative) slides into the `ConstantBlock(15)`. The `ConstantBlock(15)` then dynamically *transforms* its internal number from `15` to `8`.
    *   **Equation Update:** The `ConstantBlock(8)` settles into place.
5.  **Final Solution Display (44-50s):**
    *   **Visual:** The `EqualsBar(=)` repositions slightly to perfectly center between `VariableBox(x)` and `ConstantBlock(8)`. Both `VariableBox(x)` and `ConstantBlock(8)` pulse with a victorious, brighter glow.
    *   **Text Overlay:** "x = 8" (Appears boldly and prominently below the final equation).

---

**Phase 4: Conclusion & Verification (Checking the Work)**

1.  **Stating the Answer (50-54s):**
    *   **Visual:** The `VariableBox(x)` and `ConstantBlock(8)` separate slightly. `ConstantBlock(8)` then shifts gracefully to the center of the whiteboard.
    *   **Text Overlay:** "The number is **8**." (Fades in large and clear).
2.  **Reforming for Verification (54-58s):**
    *   **Visual:** The original equation `_ + 7 = 15` forms again on the whiteboard.
    *   **Motion:** The `VariableBox(x)` smoothly transforms into a `ConstantBlock(8)` and slides into the correct position.
    *   **Equation Display:** `8 + 7 = 15` is now displayed.
    *   **Text Overlay:** "Check Your Work: 8 + 7 = 15"
3.  **Performing the Check (58-62s):**
    *   **Visual:** The `ConstantBlock(8)` and `OperationArrow(➕)` and `ConstantBlock(7)` on the left side glow.
    *   **Motion:** `ConstantBlock(8)` and `ConstantBlock(7)` smoothly merge or combine into a new `ConstantBlock(15)` on the left side of the `EqualsBar(=)`.
    *   **Equation Display:** `15 = 15` is now clearly shown.
4.  **Confirmation (62-67s):**
    *   **Visual:** Both `ConstantBlock(15)` (left) and `ConstantBlock(15)` (right) glow, indicating perfect balance and equality.
    *   **Motion:** A large, radiant green `CheckmarkIcon` flies in from the top right, spins, and settles directly over the `15 = 15` equation, then slowly fades out.
    *   **Text Overlay:** "Solution Confirmed!" (Appears with a triumphant flourish).

---

**Phase 5: Outro (67-72s)**

1.  **Scene Fade (67-70s):**
    *   **Visual:** All elements on the whiteboard gracefully fade out, leaving just the `WhiteboardGrid` and the blurred `ClassroomScene`.
    *   **Motion:** Ambient classroom sound fades.
2.  **Branding (70-72s):**
    *   **Text Overlay:** "🎓 Cinematic Blender Animation for Math Education" or your preferred branding.
    *   **Visual:** Fades out to black.

### 🧱 Asset Suggestions

*   **VariableBox(x):**
    *   **Model:** A perfectly clean cube, slightly beveled edges.
    *   **Material:** Semi-transparent frosted glass (light blue tint), with a glowing emissive material for the 'x' on its front face (bright white or light yellow). The 'x' itself should be a bold, sans-serif font, slightly extruded.
    *   **Special Effect:** Subtle internal glow, slight rotation when isolated.

*   **ConstantBlock(N):**
    *   **Model:** A rectangular cuboid, slightly smaller than VariableBox(x).
    *   **Material:** Translucent, polished glass (e.g., orange for positive, blue for negative/final answer). The number 'N' etched within, glowing slightly.
    *   **Special Effect:** Number can smoothly morph (e.g., 15 to 8).

*   **OperationArrow(➕, ➖):**
    *   **Model:** A sleek, minimalist 3D arrow composed of glowing lines forming the symbol.
    *   **Material:** Highly emissive, vibrant green for `+`, bright red for `-`.
    *   **Special Effect:** Motion blur on movement, glow intensity changes to highlight, short trails behind.

*   **EqualsBar(=):**
    *   **Model:** Two thin, parallel 3D bars.
    *   **Material:** Shimmering gold or silver metallic, with a faint emissive glow.
    *   **Special Effect:** Can expand slightly to emphasize its role as a balance.

*   **Background:**
    *   **WhiteboardGrid:** A simple plane with a clean white texture and subtle, light grey grid lines. Good lighting to avoid flatness.
    *   **ClassroomScene:** A low-poly or blurred volumetric background of a classroom (desks, chairs, a window with soft light coming through). Crucial for context without distracting.

*   **Support Assets:**
    *   **QuestionMarkIcon:** A stylized, slightly playful 3D question mark, semi-transparent white with a light blue glow.
    *   **CheckmarkIcon:** A bold, satisfying 3D checkmark, bright green emissive material.
    *   **Particle Systems:** For "poof" (cancellation) or "sparkle" (transformation).
    *   **Text Overlays:** Clear, easy-to-read sans-serif font, consistent color (e.g., dark grey or black for general text, white for emphasized text).

---

### ⏱️ Timing and Transitions

*   **Overall Pace:** Smooth, deliberate, and clear. Each operation should have enough time to be absorbed by the viewer. Pauses are essential after major transformations.
*   **Micro-Animations (1-3 seconds):** Most individual movements (an object sliding, a number changing, an effect playing) should occur within this range for snappy yet understandable transitions.
*   **Holding States (2-5 seconds):** After a new equation or a simplified state is achieved, hold the visual for a few seconds to allow comprehension before moving to the next step.
*   **Scene Transitions (0.5-1.5 seconds):** Fade-ins/outs for text, subtle camera pushes/pulls, or quick wipes for scene changes.

This plan aims to make the abstract concept of algebra concrete and engaging through clear, purposeful animation, guiding students through each step of solving the equation.
